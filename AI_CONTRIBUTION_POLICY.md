# AI contribution policy

AI tools may assist with documentation, tests, debugging ideas, refactoring proposals, or implementation. Human contributors remain responsible for understanding, reviewing, testing, licensing, and approving the final change.

Do not submit proprietary code, confidential information, credentials, private endpoints, personal data, or material with unknown provenance to an AI tool or to this repository. Do not use AI assistance to conceal copied material or imply that an AI system is a copyright holder or contributor.

For material AI-assisted changes, disclose the assistance in the pull request and describe the human review and validation performed.
